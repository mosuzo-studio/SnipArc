SnipArc Capture Playbook — Sample Document

This is a neutral sample document used only as on-screen content for
product screenshots. It contains no personal data.

1. Press F1 to start a capture
   Hover a window and SnipArc snaps the selection to its edges. Use the
   magnifier to nudge a boundary one pixel at a time.

2. Annotate without leaving the capture
   Rectangle, arrow, pen, highlighter, step numbers, text and mosaic all
   live on the toolbar that follows the selection.

3. Scroll a long page into one image
   Switch to scrolling capture, roll the page, and adjacent frames are
   matched and blended as you go.

4. Keep a reference in view
   Press F3 to pin the capture above every other window. Scale it, fade
   it, or let the mouse click straight through it.

5. Read the text inside an image
   On-device OCR turns the characters in a capture back into selectable
   text. Nothing leaves the machine.

Release checklist
  - Verify capture overlay on every display
  - Verify annotation undo and redo
  - Verify clipboard hand-off to other applications
  - Verify portable mode writes beside the executable
